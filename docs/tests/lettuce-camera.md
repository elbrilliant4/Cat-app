# Lettuce tries the camera (build 323)

Link: https://jvtomax99.github.io/Cat-app/
Her own phone. Don't show her how first. Just give her each task and watch.

1. "Go look at Mochi's food bowl, up close."
2. "Zoom in on Mochi's face."
3. "Look out the window."
4. "Now get back to seeing the whole room."

For each one, write down:
- Did she do it? How long did it take (about)?
- What did she try first? (one finger, two fingers, tapping, the buttons)
- Did she get stuck, sigh, or say something? Write her exact words.
- Did the view ever jump, end up facing a wall, or zoom somewhere she didn't mean?

Last question for her: "What was the most annoying part?"
